Information theory is the mathematical study of the quantification, storage, and communication of information. Founded by [[claude-shannon]] in 1948, it underlies modern data compression, error correction, and cryptography.

## Key ideas

- Entropy as a measure of uncertainty.
- The bit as the fundamental unit of information.
- Channel capacity as the upper bound on reliable communication rate.
