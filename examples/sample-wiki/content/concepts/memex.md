The memex is a hypothetical electromechanical device proposed by [[vannevar-bush]] in 1945 to store and cross-link an individual's books, records, and communications. It is widely cited as a conceptual ancestor of hypertext and personal knowledge management.

## Mechanism

Bush envisioned a desk-sized microfilm reader where users could mark passages, build associative trails between documents, and share trails with others — anticipating the wikilink-style links this very wiki uses.
