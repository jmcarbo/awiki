Vannevar Bush (1890-1974) was an American engineer, inventor, and science administrator best known for his administrative role in the development of the atomic bomb and his 1945 essay "As We May Think," which proposed the [[memex]] — a hypothetical proto-hypertext device that anticipated personal computing and the World Wide Web.

## Roles

- Director of the U.S. Office of Scientific Research and Development during WWII.
- Author of [[s-as-we-may-think]] (Atlantic, 1945).
- Conceptual originator of [[memex]].
