"As We May Think" is a 1945 essay by [[vannevar-bush]] published in The Atlantic. It introduces the [[memex]] and argues that postwar science should turn to organizing the explosion of recorded human knowledge.

## Key takeaways

- Knowledge growth is outpacing human ability to consult it; mechanical aids are needed.
- The associative trail (what a researcher links together) is more useful than indexed retrieval alone.
- Sketches the [[memex]] as a personal microfilm-based hypertext machine.
